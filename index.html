How do I get these files?
  1. In Instagram, open Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  2. Choose Followers and following, pick JSON (or HTML) and request the download.
  3. When Instagram emails you, download and unzip the file.
  4. Open connections → followers_and_following and select followers_1 (add more if you have followers_2, etc.) and following.

Files are read on this device only. Nothing is uploaded. Saved results stay in this browser.
